Users & Access

Add people and choose what each of them can do

Add User

Administrator (Director) — sees everything, manages users and approves / rejects / voids payment vouchers, but cannot edit other data.
Accountant (Main Accountant) — sees and edits everything; records the cash he has at hand, money given to the Expense Accountant, his own spending and reimbursements.
Expense Accountant — sees the Expenses and Accountability sections; records expenses, vouchers and individuals, and confirms funds received.
Viewer — sees everything, edits nothing.

Sync To Calendar

Signing in keeps your shared data in sync across every device. "Connect Calendar" is optional and separate: it pushes every non-cleared cheque as a same-day event to whichever calendar you pick above, kept in sync as you add, edit, clear or delete cheques. This needs a Firebase project (see firebaseConfig near the top of the <script> section) and Calendar API access enabled for that same project. Browser security means calendar access doesn't survive a page reload — you'll be asked to sign in again if you come back later and it shows "Not connected."

Factory reset

Erases every cheque, expense, voucher, money record, person, receipt and history entry for everyone and starts the system fresh. User accounts and roles are kept. You must enter the reset password.

Reset password

This password is asked for before a Factory Reset. Keep it safe — only the Administrator should know it.

Factory Reset

This permanently deletes, for every user:
  • All customer and supplier cheques, the Trash, and the saved company and bank lists
  • All expenses, vouchers, money records, people, pay-backs and receipt photos
  • The full history log, and the voucher and transaction numbering (starts again from 1)
Kept: user accounts and roles, and the reset password.
